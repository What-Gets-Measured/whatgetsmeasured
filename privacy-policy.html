What Gets Measured is operated by What Gets Measured LLC ("we," "us," "our"), a Florida limited liability company. This policy explains what information we collect through whatgetsmeasured.com, how we use it, and the choices you have — regardless of what state or country you're visiting from.

Information We Collect

Right now, this site does not use a contact form, analytics, or tracking cookies. Information is only collected when you reach out to us directly — for example, by emailing us or booking a consultation through our scheduling tool. That may include your name, email address, business name, and anything else you choose to share with us.

If we add tools to the site in the future that collect information automatically (such as analytics or a contact form), we'll update this policy to describe what's collected and why before those tools go live.

How We Use It

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

When you book a consultation, you may be directed to a third-party scheduling tool (such as Google Calendar) to select a time. That service has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your information, separate from ours.

Data From Client Work

If you become a client, any business data you share with us as part of a consulting engagement or custom tool build — such as job records, customer lists, or sales history — is used solely to do that work. We don't reference, publish, or share specifics about your business publicly without your explicit permission first.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws like the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar state privacy laws, including the right to know what information we have about you, request its deletion, or opt out of certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

This site and our services are intended for business owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
